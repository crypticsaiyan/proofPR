## 2. What ProofPR does

Bug reports posted in Discord become either a pull request that carries its own
proof, or an honest decline with a reason code. It acts across Discord,
Linear, and GitHub, writes to all three, and reads back every write.

## 4. Why the proof is the product

The reproduction gate, the revert check, the flake check, the diff-scoped
mutation check, and the project's own CI decide whether work is real. For real
historical bugs, the upstream maintainer's own test, which the agent never sees,
decides correctness. There is no model judging model output anywhere in this
system.

## 5. Consistency and recovery

Readback after every write, a three-way consistency assertion across the three
applications at every terminal state, a drift reconciler every six hours, and a
hash-chained receipt published in three places.
